# Licensing guidance

Original NOXUS CORE code, packaged templates, and generated project source are licensed
GPL-3.0-or-later. Dependencies keep their own licenses. Frappe is used under its license and ERPNext
is an optional GPL-covered application; NOXUS never edits ERPNext core files.

Third-party notices and source offers must accompany distributed artifacts when their licenses
require them. Future proprietary network services must remain separately deployed and must not copy
GPL source into a closed component.

This document is engineering guidance, not legal advice. Distributors should obtain qualified legal
review for their exact distribution and hosting model.
